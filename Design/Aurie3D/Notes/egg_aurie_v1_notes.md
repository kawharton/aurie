## 1. Frozen / approved — body

Egg-specific three-term profile `egg_width(t) = BODY_WIDEN · belly ·
crown · side` with `belly = 1 + BELLY_FULL·u_bot^BELLY_EASE`,
`crown = 1 − TOP_TAPER·u_top^TAPER_EASE`, `side = 1 +
SIDE_FILL·t²(1−t²)` — an INDEPENDENTLY TUNABLE body architecture. Egg
is NOT generated by stretching Round or shortening Tall; that
principle is part of the frozen architecture.

- `EGG_STRETCH 1.08`, `BODY_WIDEN 0.92`
- `BELLY_FULL 0.16`, `BELLY_EASE 1.3`
- `TOP_TAPER 0.16`, `TAPER_EASE 1.7`
- `SIDE_FILL 0.08` (deliberately low — continuously changing width)
- `BOTTOM_FLATTEN 0.015`, `BOTTOM_FLAT_START -0.5`
- `BODY_LIFT 1.074` (bottom at +0.010)

Approved dimensions: body ≈ **2.144 × 1.892 R, H/W ≈ 1.13** (Round
0.96, Small 1.06, Tall 1.32, Wide 0.79). **Maximum width ≈ 0.20 R
below the geometric center** (t ≈ −0.18, world z 0.881 vs center
1.082) — PRESERVE this relationship. Crown vs mirrored base
half-widths: −15.3% at |t| 0.5, −20.7% at 0.7, −24.3% at 0.85. Normal
family world scale (not smaller like Small): height +10.5% / width
−6.3% vs Round; authoritative world bounds incl. limbs/tuft
**2.39 × 2.22 R** (verifier alpha audit).

**MUST NOT DRIFT** — the balance is intentional and approved; do NOT
increase bottom heaviness or top taper further:

- NOT toward Pear: no stronger hip/belly dominance, no dramatically
  smaller upper body.
- NOT toward Teardrop: no pointed end, no droplet silhouette.
- NOT toward Tall: no sustained capsule-like sidewalls.
- NOT toward Round: do not redistribute the maximum width back toward
  the center.

## 8. Development notes / history

No EGG panel exists in `References/aurie_body_types_reference.png`
(only the original five) — v1 was built from the written spec. Design
constraints: classic soft egg, MODERATE by design so the neighbors
stay distinct (Pear = much more bottom-dominant, future body;
Teardrop = tapered tip, future body; Tall = sustained flanks, frozen;
Round = center-weighted width, frozen). Normal family world scale.

v1 process: the profile constants were validated ANALYTICALLY before
any render (scratchpad precheck of the true silhouette curve
sqrt(1−t²)·egg_width(t): max-width location, H/W, crown/base ratios,
anchor-height flank widths, framing) — the first rendered candidate
passed body-only review with no internal variants needed. Face/limb
anchors derived at normalized family positions (eyes ~63% up, arms
~36% up, feet under base). Approved on first review with the explicit
instruction: do not increase bottom heaviness or top taper further.
